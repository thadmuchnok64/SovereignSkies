# Wyverncloth Jacket

A royal blue jacket made of extremely thin and dense feathers. Looks light, but is fairly sturdy material. The inner part of the jacket is lined with a stretchy black leather/skin. 
The back of the jacket is lined with a long skinny light blue [[Wyvern]] flying up the spine of the shirt, with long wavy wings which fade from blue into white. Has green buttons sewn onto one side.

This is probably the comfiest thing [[Finnegan]] has ever worn.

While falling down, the wearer can inflate the suit. The jacket becomes poofy (kinda like a puffer jacket) While falling down, the user gains +1 AC. Gravity automatically travels the user downward 5 ft every turn. The user is naturally immune to fall damage while the jacket is inflated.

 +Lightning Resistance
 +User gains one more 1st level spell slot
 +Counts as winter clothes
