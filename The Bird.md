A clockwork mechanism bird. Made of wood, brass, and glass, with a diamond in the center of the chest. The body of the bird is splitin half by a moving gear.

HP: 8/8
AC: 16 
Speed: 45 ft (flying)

Throws:
STR: -2
DEX: +4
CON: -2
WIS: Immune
INT: Immune
CHR: Immune

Ammo per long rest:
3/3 magical bombs

Every long rest, a party member can become mentally linked to the bird, and control them for that day.
A familiar which can fly around, capture images, and display then via a minor illusion rune. Can only store a maximum of three images at a time.
The bird cannot fly farther than 500 feet from the user it is bound to, or else the bird will go into a self preservation mode, and seek the user. If the bird is unable to get back to the bound user, it will become dormant.
Makes a low humming sound when flying around, which makes it difficult for close quarters stealth reconnaissance for quiet environments.
Can drop 3 bombs each long rest. The bombs drop directly below unless there are affected by winds. (Wind conditions may affect your ability to hit). The bound user must make an attack roll using their wisdom
The bombs hit a target for 1d6 bludgeoning damage. and explode in a radius of 5 feet
All afflicted creatures in the proximity must make a DEX >= 14 save or suffer 1d4 fire damage.

When the bird's HP falls to 0, it becomes a dormant object. The crystal is shattered and must be replaced with another small-sized crystal, by an artificer.
