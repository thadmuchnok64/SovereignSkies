A group of dwarf vikings
Leader: [[Poindexter]]

Members:
[[Hagdarr]] (the orc)

Allied to [[Elves of the Forest]]

Started out purely as Vikings who would raid anyone and everyone.
Descended from the [[Southern Dwarves]]

Eventually bit off too much than they could chew when they attacked the [[Elves of the Forest]] on a remote island. Were captured and about half of their members were killed during the fight.
[[Poindexter]] emerged as the leader as the old one was killed.

Poindexter decided to have the group slowly earn the respect of the elves by doing work for them, slowly building up trust.
One day, they were instructed to slay an evil orc in a cave. They took on the job, but instead of killing them, they befriended him, and the orc joined the crew as an honorary dwarf.

Finally, a few days prior to [[The Pirate Crew]] arriving on their island, they were there to slaughter the monkey cultist encampment there.
