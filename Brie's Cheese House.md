A cheese shop run by [[Brie]] that is near between [[Ashford University District]] and [[Scallywag's Alley]]

The cheese house is structured as follows:

[Top Floor] : The main home of [[Brie]]'s children. Has a main bedroom, and a bedroom for all the children. Has a kitchen and a small living room.

[Ground Floor] : The main storefront. The front door is locked during non-store hours. A spare key is stored under a loose wooden plank on the front porch.
Inside, there are kegs full of milk. Various expensive spices, and a cheese menu.

The floor is wooden and rather creaky, so the person sneaking through needs to be really quiet.

during non-store hours, the cheese carts are wheeled into the cheese vault.

Behind the counter, there is a hidden door in the wall which leads to a ramp that goes down into the lower floor which is the cheese vault.

The hidden door has another door right behind it which has a big fancy lock on it.
[Brie] holds the lock around her neck. She wears it to sleep.

[Underground] : The main cheese vault. There are various cheeses from all over the hemisphere stored here. There is a main cheese coated in goat that seems to be behind glass.
