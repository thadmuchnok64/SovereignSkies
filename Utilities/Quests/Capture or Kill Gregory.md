
- [ ] The Queen's Speech ⏫ 

Horns in the air are blaring. Everyone is sullen. The protests that the party have seen not too long ago have quieted down. All the folk near the city hall seem much more depressed. They lost their fire.

A tall yellow elf with long ears and ornately braided hair named [[Eleanor]] walks out in front of the people, on top of the marble platform above.

"Greetings citizens. Thank you all for joining us on this dark day. It pains us to announce that [[King Lynell]] has been assassinated in the [[Banana Islands]]...

Unfortunately, our good king only had one naturally born heir... Young [[Prince Linus]]. He unfortunately also was on the caravan to the Banana Islands."

She paused for a moment. She then reaches into her breastpocket and pulls out a note.

We received a letter merely moments ago.

"Your king has been crucified, dismembered, and burnt to cinders. Now his crown rests upon my head. You know, I have to admit. Your king is a very eloquent speaker. I was quite moved by his powerful speech, I must say.
I also have the prince in my custody.
If you ever want him to return home, send a galleon full of gold to the banana isles, or else Linus will suffer an even worse fate than his fathers.
Send me the gold, and your prince will return home safely

You have until the first of Amberdawn to accomodate these requests.

-Lots of Love, G. E. Gobblemore"

We do not intend to negotiate with this monster.

We have decided to place a bounty on the head of this "G. E. Gobblemore, and another bounty on our beloved Prince."

The prince must be returned alive. for Gobblemore... bring us his head.

Completing this task will provide you with a sum of gold, and property within the city, along with  favor from the new King

Thank you all.

*She bows* and returns into the ornate gate behind her.
