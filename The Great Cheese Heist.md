After the cheese is aquired, [[Melvin]] will show [[Finnegan]] the process for fishing for [[Busterdust]].

Step 1: Grab a rat.
Step 2: Dunk the rat into a bucket of water.
Step 3: Throw the wet rat into a room with a barrel of [[Busterdust]]
Step 4: Attach cheese to a fishing hook.
Step 5: Bait the rat into the [[Busterdust]]. The busterdust will completely coat the wet rat.
Step 6: Have the rat bite onto the hook and reel them up.
Step 7: Infinite busterdust.
